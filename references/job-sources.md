# Vacancy sources and remote-work verification

Read this reference when finding vacancies, evaluating a linked role, or preparing to apply. A job board is a discovery source, not proof that a vacancy is current, authentic, or available from the candidate's country.

## Authoritative sources

Choose sources that match the candidate's geography and target market. For a fully remote international search, start with a focused selection from this list rather than searching all sources indiscriminately.

| Source | Best use | Required check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [LinkedIn Jobs](https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/) | Broad international market and recruiter outreach | Apply the Remote filter, then read the posting for permitted worker locations; a Remote label alone is insufficient. |
| [We Work Remotely](https://weworkremotely.com/) | Established remote-first companies and technology roles | Prefer `Worldwide` or `Anywhere in the World`; reject region-only listings that exclude the candidate. |
| [Remotive](https://remotive.com/remote-world-jobs?locations=Worldwide) | Curated remote roles, particularly in technology | Use the `Worldwide` filter and confirm the same scope in the original company posting. |
| [Working Nomads](https://www.workingnomads.com/) | Curated fully remote digital roles | Use location `Anywhere`; verify whether the employer still limits payroll or residence countries. |
| [Wellfound](https://wellfound.com/jobs) | Startup and scale-up hiring | Select remote roles and inspect `Hires remotely from`; do not assume a remote startup hires worldwide. |
| [Djinni](https://djinni.co/jobs/) | Technology roles and direct recruiter outreach in Eastern Europe and international markets | Select remote work, then verify candidate-country eligibility, contract type, and timezone. |
| [Y Combinator Remote Jobs](https://www.ycombinator.com/jobs/role/all/remote) | Direct opportunities at YC-backed startups | Treat `Remote (US)`, `Remote (EU)`, or another named region as restricted; accept only a compatible region or worldwide scope. |
| [hh.kz](https://hh.kz/) | Remote employment for candidates based in Kazakhstan | Select remote work and verify that no office attendance, relocation, or city-specific presence is required. |
| [FlexJobs](https://www.flexjobs.com/) | Hand-screened remote and flexible work across industries | Prefer `100% Remote` and `Work from Anywhere`; note that full access may require a paid account. |
| [Himalayas](https://himalayas.app/jobs) | Large remote-job index with location and timezone filters | Select worldwide availability and confirm details on the employer's original posting. |
| [Work From Anywhere](https://workfromanywhere.app/) | Narrow search for location-independent roles | Verify that the linked first-party vacancy still has no residence restriction. |

## First-party verification

Before recommending or applying, locate the same vacancy on the company's official career site. Common legitimate ATS hosts include:

- `jobs.ashbyhq.com`;
- `job-boards.greenhouse.io`;
- `jobs.lever.co`;
- a company-specific `myworkdayjobs.com` subdomain;
- the company's own verified domain.

An ATS domain is not sufficient by itself: verify that the company identity, role title, location, requisition, and application link agree with the official company site. Prefer the first-party version when an aggregator and the company posting differ.

## Fully remote eligibility

`Remote` describes the workplace, not necessarily the countries from which a person may work. Classify the vacancy before presenting it as suitable.

### Pass

All of the following are supported by the posting or confirmed by the employer:

- work is fully remote with no routine office attendance;
- the candidate's country is explicitly eligible, or the role says `Worldwide` or `Anywhere` without a conflicting restriction;
- employment is possible through a local entity, Employer of Record, or an international contractor agreement;
- required working hours and on-call coverage are compatible with the candidate's timezone;
- mandatory travel or in-person events match the user's stated limits.

### Needs clarification

Do not treat these as worldwide eligibility without confirmation:

- `EMEA`, `Europe`, `APAC`, or another region that does not explicitly resolve whether the candidate's country is included;
- `remote-first`, `distributed`, `work from home`, or `flexible location` without permitted countries;
- an unspecified payroll country or work-authorization requirement;
- occasional office visits, retreats, customer travel, or an unclear on-call window.

Ask one concise question or mark the vacancy as conditional.

### Reject for a strict fully remote search

- hybrid or onsite work;
- mandatory office attendance, even periodically, when the user requires zero office visits;
- a residence or work-authorization restriction that excludes the candidate;
- relocation as a condition of employment;
- required in-person activity outside the user's accepted travel limit.

## Vacancy checks

Record and verify:

1. **Source and freshness:** original posting URL, publication or update date when available, and whether applications remain open.
2. **Employer identity:** company domain, consistent company name, real product or business, and recruiter communication from an expected domain.
3. **Remote scope:** fully remote versus hybrid; eligible countries; location exclusions; office, retreat, and travel requirements.
4. **Engagement:** employee, Employer of Record, or contractor; contract duration; probation; exclusivity; notice period.
5. **Timezone:** required overlap, core hours, night work, on-call rotation, and daylight-saving implications.
6. **Compensation:** currency, gross or net basis, employment versus contractor treatment, payment method, and location-based adjustment when stated.
7. **Equipment and access:** who supplies equipment, monitoring or camera requirements, secure workspace, VPN, or location-specific access rules.
8. **Application safety:** no candidate payment, equipment purchase, money transfer, cryptocurrency transaction, or collection of unnecessary identity or banking data before a legitimate offer and onboarding process.

If a job-board copy omits any material condition, inspect the first-party vacancy or mark the condition unknown. Never convert an unknown into an assumption.

## Presenting search results

For each recommended vacancy, report compactly:

- company and role;
- discovery source and first-party application link;
- evidence for fully remote status;
- eligible country or region;
- contract or EOR information when stated;
- timezone and travel requirements;
- material unknowns or red flags;
- why the verified candidate profile matches.

Do not include a role in a strict `100% remote from my country` shortlist when geographic eligibility or office attendance remains unresolved. Put it in a separate `Needs confirmation` section instead.
